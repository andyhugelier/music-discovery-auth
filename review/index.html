How these groups are formed

These are broad genre and tag groups, not learned clusters. Track genres are used first. Where those are missing, artist tags or genres from other library tracks by the artist provide a provisional estimate. They describe style, not your mood or a confirmed preference.

Source tracks remain separate across services. Counts are not unique recordings or independent plays. A saved song alone is weak evidence; explicit preferences and repeated listening guide the representative examples.
